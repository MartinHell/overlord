Weapons

Hits per shot is a ratio and can exceed 1 legitimately — DCS reports one shot per launch but one hit per object damaged, so cluster and splash weapons register several. Guns report hits and no shots, so they have no ratio. Rows named after an aircraft are collisions, where DCS names the aircraft as the weapon; those are counted apart from hits and kills so they do not flatter any weapon's figures.
