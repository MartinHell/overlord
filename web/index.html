Weapon effectiveness

Rows named after an airframe rather than a weapon are collisions: DCS reports the aircraft as the weapon when one is flown into something. Ratios, not percentages, and they legitimately exceed 1: DCS emits one shot event per launch but one hit event per object damaged, so submunition and splash weapons register several hits from a single shot. Hits on scenery are excluded. Guns emit hits but no shot events, so anything with no shots shows —.
