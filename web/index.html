    The map

    Every geolocated kill in scope, both sides. Roughly half of kills carry no position and cannot be shown.

    Weapons

    Hits per shot is a ratio and can exceed 1 legitimately — DCS reports one shot per launch but one hit per object damaged, so cluster and splash weapons register several. Guns report hits and no shots, so they have no ratio. Rows named after an aircraft are collisions, where DCS names the aircraft as the weapon.
